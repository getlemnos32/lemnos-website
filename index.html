The problem

The paperwork decides whether you get paid.

Specialty practices lose hours every week to work that has nothing to do with patient care, and every miss shows up later as a delay, a write-off, or a denial.

Eligibility

Coverage, benefits, and network status confirmed by hand before every visit, often on the phone, often at the last minute.

Prior authorization

Clinical notes pulled together, forms filled out for each payer, then days of follow-up calls and portal checks until a decision comes back.

Denials

Claims come back denied for missing documentation or a coding mismatch, and the appeal lands on the same overloaded staff.

How the desk works

Agents do the legwork. Your team makes the call.

01

Verify

Eligibility and benefits checked ahead of the visit, with anything that needs attention flagged for your staff.

02

Assemble

The prior auth packet built from the chart: the right forms, the right clinical documentation, for the right payer.

03

Chase

Follow-up with the payer until there's a decision, so requests stop sitting in a queue nobody is watching.

04

Appeal

When a claim is denied, the appeal is drafted with the supporting documentation, ready for your review.

Nothing goes to a payer until a person on your team says yes.
